# [XAS-074] A title or reason with ' · ' on the board stays whole

**Status**: Open
**Priority**: Low
**Component**: `plugins/xxthunder-dev-skills/scripts/board.py`, ADR-0017
**Related**: [XAS-060](xas-060.md)

**Summary**:
As the author, I want a board card whose title or reason contains ` · ` to keep both whole when it moves or leaves the board, so that a commit subject and a moved card never lose text.

**Description**:
`board.py` (XAS-060) tells a card's title from its reason at the last ` · ` when the title has drifted from the item, and the `title:` line it prints for a close or drop does the same. A title or Jira summary that contains ` · ` and has no reason is then cut there: the commit subject loses its tail, and a drifted title leaves its tail behind as a made-up reason. ADR-0017 records this as a known limit. A fix needs a separator a title cannot contain, or a reason marker in the card format — a format change, so the store's `Board` contract follows.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] A card whose title or summary contains ` · ` keeps its title whole on move, close and drop, with or without a reason
- [ ] A drifted title with ` · ` and no reason gains no reason when it is refreshed
- [ ] ADR-0017's consequence about the lossy split is updated or superseded
