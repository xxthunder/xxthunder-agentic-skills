# [XAS-042] `next` does not report the standing refinement item

**Status**: In Progress
**Priority**: Medium
**Component**: `plugins/xxthunder-dev-skills/scripts/backlog.py`,
`tests/dev/scripts/test_backlog.py`,
`plugins/xxthunder-dev-skills/skills/next/SKILL.md`,
`plugins/xxthunder-dev-skills/.claude-plugin/plugin.json`,
`.claude-plugin/marketplace.json`
**Related**: [XAS-040](xas-040.md) (`next` and its contradiction checks),
[XAS-035](xas-035.md) (`logbook`, whose open starts are *in progress*)

**Summary**:
As a maintainer reading `/next`, I want the standing refinement item of each
backlog left out of the contradiction report, so that the report lists only
what I have to act on.

**Description**:
`refinement` gives every backlog an ongoing item titled *Backlog refinement*,
In Progress and never completed. `next show` reports every item that is In
Progress in its backlog without an open start in the log, so the first run
across repos reported one refinement item per backlog, in every run, with
nothing to do about it.

The refinement item is not work that starts and ends, and the log does not
carry it: a `started` line would never get its `done`, and would list the item
under *In progress* forever.

Until now the check was prose in `next`'s `SKILL.md`, the epic exclusion
included: the agent applied it by hand, and no test held it.

**Scope Decisions** (author, 2026-10-21):
- **The log does not carry refinement items**, and `next` does not report them
  as contradictions — the same kind of exclusion as for epics.
- **Recognised by its title**: the item titled *Backlog refinement*, the title
  `refinement` gives it. Its priority does not identify it — most carry `—`,
  some a real priority.
- **The backlog half of the check is code** (author, 2026-10-22):
  `backlog.py show --in-progress` prints the items that are In Progress as
  work — epics and the refinement item left out — with tests. Comparing them
  with the log stays with the agent: the log's format is the store's contract,
  and the plugin ships none of its own.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] `backlog.py show --in-progress` prints only In Progress items, leaving out
      an item with substories and the item titled *Backlog refinement*; it
      works with `--repo` and with IDs
- [ ] `next` takes the In Progress items for the contradiction check from
      `show --in-progress`; its `SKILL.md` no longer states the exclusions
- [ ] Every other contradiction check is unchanged
- [ ] `plugin.json` and `marketplace.json` bumped (minor)
- [ ] Verified across repos: `/next` reports none of the refinement items
