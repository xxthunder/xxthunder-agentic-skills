# [XAS-077] The ADR log can record that a decision amends part of an earlier one

**Status**: Open
**Priority**: Low
**Component**: `docs/adr/README.md`, ADR-0008, ADR-0011, ADR-0017
**Related**: [XAS-060](xas-060.md)

**Summary**:
As the maintainer, I want the ADR log to say when a decision amends part of an earlier one, so that reading ADR-0008 or ADR-0011 leads to ADR-0017 instead of to a statement that is no longer true.

**Description**:
ADR-0017 (XAS-060) moves the board's format from the store's contract into `board.py`. It amends part of ADR-0008 (the store's `AGENTS.md` is the contract) and ADR-0011 (`backlog-ops` moves the card), but the ADR log only knows full supersession: both still read Accepted with no pointer to 0017, and 0011 still says the format is the contract's.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] `docs/adr/README.md` states how a partial amendment is recorded
- [ ] ADR-0008 and ADR-0011 point to ADR-0017 for the part it changes
