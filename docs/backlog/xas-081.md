# [XAS-081] The evidence package summarises the change and the gate as the contract says

**Status**: Open
**Priority**: Medium
**Component**: `plugins/xxthunder-dev-skills/skills/closed-loop/SKILL.md` (Package, step 3)
**Related**: [XAS-055](xas-055.md) (the closed loop)

**Summary**:
As the author, I want the evidence package to summarise the change and the gate in a few
sentences, so that the pull request description says what I need to know and the commits keep
the details.

**Description**:
On 2026-11-08 the author reviewed the evidence package of shortcuts #105 (SC-086): *What
changes* listed every commit with its reason, and *Gate* told the whole story of every run.
Both repeat what the commits and the CI already hold. The author keeps *Item*, *Review* and
*Your steps* as they are.

The contract `Closed loop` in the author's store now says (2026-11-08):

- **What changes** — a short summary of the change as a whole, in a few sentences; the
  commits carry the details.
- **Gate** — in a few sentences: what the gate is and why, what ran locally and why, and the
  result.

The skill's Package step 3 still prescribes the old content: *What changes* "one line per
commit of `git log <base>..HEAD`, with its reason", and *Gate* "the three recorded facts — the
command, the contract's line for its source, where it ran — and the result: exit code, and the
output's tail on failure". The skill says the sections are filled "as the contract describes",
but the explicit per-section lines win in practice: the next loop writes the long version again.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] Package step 3 no longer prescribes the content of *What changes* and *Gate*; it fills both as the contract describes them
- [ ] An eval case fails against `develop` (the package lists every commit, or tells the gate's story run by run) and passes after the change
- [ ] The plugin version is bumped in `plugin.json` and `marketplace.json`
